## About

I am a BCA final-year student focused on **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Cloud Computing**.

My work focuses on designing and building practical AI systems that combine:

- Machine Learning and Computer Vision
- Large Language Models and Generative AI
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Agentic AI and intelligent workflows
- AWS cloud services and architecture
- MLOps and model lifecycle management
- AIOps and distributed observability

I enjoy taking ideas from **prototype to working systems**, with an emphasis on reliability, scalability, and real-world usability.
