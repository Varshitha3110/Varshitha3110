## About Me

I am a BCA final-year student focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI and Cloud Computing.

My work focuses on building practical AI systems that combine machine learning, large language models, cloud services and modern software engineering practices.

My areas of interest include:

- Generative AI and Large Language Models
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Agentic AI and AI workflows
- Amazon Bedrock and AWS cloud architecture
- Machine Learning and Computer Vision
- MLOps and model lifecycle management
- AIOps and distributed observability

My goal is to build reliable, scalable and useful AI applications that solve real-world problems.
